## Workshop release

`addons/30` started as the RPG-30 Hook Workshop release (item 3042754975). The
model `rpg_30.p3d`, its textures and materials, the UI icons, the sound
`sound/double.ogg` and the pictures in `data` came with that release without a
recorded source. Their authors and licenses are to be confirmed.

The decoy round uses the vanilla `Rocket_01_fly_F` model and the PG-30 the
vanilla RPG-32 rocket model; neither is redistributed.
